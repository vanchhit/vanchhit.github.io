A policy framework that draws on precedents from nuclear energy licensing, pharmaceutical trials, and environmental impact assessments to design a tractable compute governance regime for frontier AI.

## The core idea

Training runs above a certain compute threshold should require a permit. The permit tier scales with the FLOP budget and triggers corresponding safety requirements: red-teaming, third-party evaluation, incident reporting.
